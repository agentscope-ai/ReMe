# Working habits

The team writes original records to files before summarizing them into durable knowledge. Internal material stays in the controlled workspace by default; sending it to an external model depends on the actual deployment and provider settings.

Lin records conclusions on the day of a meeting. Zhou checks recovery before releases. He uses field feedback to correct untested assumptions.

These habits come from [[resources/kickoff.md|kickoff agreements]] and [[resources/offline-feedback.md|field constraints]]. They help an agent understand context, while remaining inspectable, editable, and removable when outdated.
